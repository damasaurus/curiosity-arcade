Curious about the secret sauce?+

This game borrows its inspiration from research on intellectual humility: how people handle uncertainty, new evidence, and viewpoints that differ from their own.

It is not the CIHS, a validated assessment, or a judgment of anyone’s character. These quick choices only make a playful result. People react differently depending on the situation—and that’s part of being human.
